About the Research

Preserving pantun through intelligent analysis.

This research explores transformer-based approaches for automatic theme classification of Malay pantun while incorporating semantic relationship analysis to reveal relationships between poetic texts.

By combining natural language processing with Malay literary heritage, the project demonstrates how computational methods can support the exploration and understanding of traditional literature.
